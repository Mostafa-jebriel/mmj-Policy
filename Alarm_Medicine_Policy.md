# Privacy Policy for Alarm Medicine App

**Last updated:** August 09, 2026

Welcome to **Alarm Medicine** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle user data, how your information is stored, and your rights regarding data deletion.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Storage

- **Local Storage:** The app is designed primarily as a local medicine reminder. Alarm schedules, medicine names, dosages, and notification preferences are stored locally on your mobile device.
- **Personal Data:** We do not collect, trade, or share your personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location) on external servers without your explicit consent.
- **Third-Party Services:** If the app utilizes third-party tools (such as Google Play Services, AdMob, or Firebase Analytics for crash reports), these services may collect basic diagnostic information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

---

## 2. User Rights & Data Deletion Process (سياسة حذف البيانات)

Google Play policy requires us to provide clear instructions on how you can request the deletion of your data. 

### A. Automatic Local Data Deletion:
Since most of your app data (medicine lists, schedules, and app settings) is stored locally on your device, you can delete all stored data immediately at any time by:
1. Clearing the app data from your phone settings: **Settings > Apps > Alarm Medicine > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage**.
2. Uninstalling the app from your device, which automatically removes all locally saved data.

### B. Requesting Manual Data Deletion:
If you wish to submit a formal request to delete any associated data or account information (if applicable):
1. **Send an Email:** Send an email to **your-email@example.com** with the subject line `Data Deletion Request - Alarm Medicine`.
2. **Details Required:** Please mention the email address or device ID associated with your app usage (if applicable).
3. **Processing Time:** We will process your deletion request and confirm the removal of any stored or associated data within **7 to 14 business days**.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.
